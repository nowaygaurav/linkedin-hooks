# 10 hooks i actually posted

A free, interactive guide: 10 LinkedIn hooks from my own posts, ranked by reactions. Each one has the real post (screenshot + link), why it works, a template and a 5 minute task.

Live: https://nowaygaurav.github.io/linkedin-hooks/

- Fill in the "your turn" boxes, the worksheet and the checklist on the page (saved in your browser only).
- **download my pdf** builds your filled-in copy in the browser (html2canvas + jsPDF, nothing is uploaded).
- **blank pdf** is `10-hooks-i-actually-posted.pdf`.

To edit: change the `HOOKS` list in `build.py`, then run `./make-pdf.sh` (needs Brave or Chrome) and push.
Post screenshots live in `shots/`.
