read this first

your hook has one job

make them read line two. not go viral, not sound smart. just line two.

1

two lines, then a gap

line 1 is the tension, line 2 is the promise. keep it under ~15 words before “see more” cuts you off, and make line 1 fit on one phone line.

2

who + problem + hold back

name who it's for, touch a problem they actually have, and don't hand over the answer in the hook.

3

write 5, post 1

write the same hook 5 ways, each in a different style from this guide. post the one that makes you want to read the next line.

~3x

what my own data told me: posts about my actual life got 105 to 175 reactions. my “here are 5 tips” posts got 36 to 52.

(yes i checked. it hurt a little.)

so every hook in here starts with something that happened to you. not “you should”.

before you hit post

the 30 second check

  • line 1 fits on one phone line
  • under ~15 words before “see more”
  • there's a real number, name or place in it
  • i'd still stand by it if nobody reacted
  • if i pasted it on a stranger's profile, it wouldn't make sense. (that's how you know it's yours)

all 5 ticked? post it. missing one? fix line 1, not the whole post.

the one i left out

fear hooks. “if you don't use AI you'll be unemployable in 2 years.” they work. they also turn you into that guy.

i'd rather be the guy who spent 1,15,643 in july and told you exactly where it went.
